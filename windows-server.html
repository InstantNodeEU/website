Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Our support team answers on Discord and by ticket.

Is a Windows license included?
No. Every server ships with the Windows Server 2022 or 2025 evaluation edition, pre-installed and ready to use. Bring your own license key to activate it for continued use.
How do I connect to my Windows Server?
Via Remote Desktop (RDP). After deployment, you will find the IP address and administrator credentials in your dashboard. Connect from Windows, macOS, Linux or mobile RDP clients.
Can I resize my server later?
Yes. Scale up to 32 vCPU, 64 GB RAM and 1 TB of NVMe storage whenever your workload grows. You only pay for what you configure.
Why does a Windows VPS cost more than a Linux KVM VPS?
Windows needs more RAM and disk than Linux and is more demanding to run. That makes a Windows VPS about 15% more expensive than the equivalent Linux KVM configuration.
Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept PayPal, cryptocurrencies and your InstantNode account balance. Card payments are currently not available.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Our support team answers on Discord and by ticket.

How fast is my game server online?
Your server itself is provisioned in about 60 seconds after checkout - panel, files and console are ready straight away. The game files are then pulled automatically on first start, and how long that takes depends on the title: small ones like Minecraft or Terraria are ready almost immediately, larger ones such as ARK, Squad or Insurgency take a few more minutes. You can watch the progress live in the console.
Can I switch games later?
Yes. Plans are game-agnostic tiers, so any plan can run any game we offer. You pick the game when you create the server. You can redeploy it with a different game later - no plan change needed.
Can I upload my existing world or save?
Yes. Every server includes full file access via SFTP and the web file manager. That lets you upload existing worlds and saves. Palworld even has a built-in co-op save migration tool. It moves your local world onto your server directly from the panel.
Do I get a free domain for my Minecraft server?
Yes. Every paid Minecraft Java server can claim a free address under inngame.eu, for example yourname.inngame.eu. You pick the name in the panel under Network, and your players connect with just that name - no port to remember, because we set the matching SRV record for you. It is optional and free, and the plain IP address and port keep working exactly as before. Free Minecraft servers and other games are not included, because only the Minecraft Java client can look up the record that hides the port.
Do you protect against DDoS attacks?
Yes - always-on DDoS protection is included free with every game server. That means attacks are mitigated before they reach your players.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept PayPal, cryptocurrency and your InstantNode account balance. Card payments are currently not available.
Where are the servers hosted?
In the EU. Our game servers run in our Eygelshoven (Netherlands) datacenter - the location shown on every plan. It's GDPR-compliant European infrastructure.

Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Our support team answers on Discord and by ticket.

Can I install my own operating system?
Yes. Reinstall to any of our supported images: Debian 12 and 13, Ubuntu 22.04 to 26.04, Rocky Linux 9 and 10, AlmaLinux 9 and 10, CentOS Stream 9, Fedora 42, openSUSE Leap 15.6, Arch Linux or even Proxmox VE 9. Proxmox VE 9 has nested virtualization enabled, so you can run your own VMs inside your VPS. You do this straight from the control panel in a couple of clicks.
Can I resize my VPS later?
Yes. Scale up to 32 vCPU, 64 GB RAM and 1 TB of NVMe storage whenever you need to. You only pay for the resources you configure.
Is DDoS protection included?
Yes. Always-on DDoS mitigation is included with each KVM VPS at no extra cost.
Can I migrate my existing server to InstantNode?
Yes - use our free Server Importer. The wizard connects to your old server over SFTP and pulls your data onto your new VPS for you.
Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept PayPal, cryptocurrencies and your InstantNode account balance. Card payments are currently not available.

Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Our support team answers on Discord and by ticket.

How fast will my server be online?
Deployment is automated. Game servers and KVM VPS are online in about 60 seconds after checkout. Your server then runs 24/7, backed by our 99.9% uptime SLA.
Which payment methods do you accept?
You top up your InstantNode balance via PayPal, credit card or cryptocurrency. All services are then paid from that prepaid balance.
Is DDoS protection included?
Yes. Every server comes with free, always-on DDoS protection - there is nothing to configure and no extra cost.
Where are your servers located?
All servers are hosted in the Netherlands, in an EU datacenter. Data handling is GDPR-compliant, with low ping across Europe.
Can I migrate my existing server to InstantNode?
Yes - our Server Importer moves your old server for you. Pick your OS and enter your old server's SFTP login. We then move your data onto a fresh KVM VPS for you.
Can I upgrade or change my server later?
Any time. You can upgrade resources from the control panel as your project grows, reinstall or switch OS in a few clicks, and invite friends as subusers to help manage your server.
How do I get support?
The fastest way is our Discord community, where the team is active around the clock. You can also open a ticket from your dashboard, or check our documentation and status page.

We don't scatter servers across a dozen resold racks. Everything InstantNode runs - game servers, KVM VPS, web hosting and Discord bots - lives on hardware we operate in the Netherlands, so we can vouch for every layer of the stack.

Location: Netherlands, EU jurisdiction

Your services and your data are hosted in a datacenter facility in the Netherlands and never leave the European Union. That means EU law applies, and your customers' personal data is processed under the GDPR - see our privacy policy for the details. For European users it also simply means lower ping.

Hardware: AMD EPYC, NVMe, ECC

Our nodes are built around the AMD EPYC 7763 - 64 cores and 128 threads per CPU - paired with error-correcting ECC memory and enterprise NVMe storage. No spinning disks, no desktop parts. The full spec breakdown lives on our hardware page.

Network: low-latency EU routing

Sitting in the middle of Europe's internet backbone, our network is optimised for short paths to users across the EU and the UK. DDoS protection is included on every plan - game server, VPS or dedicated - at no extra charge, because we think paying extra to stay online is absurd.

Reliability: built to stay up

We target 99.9% uptime and back it with continuous monitoring, redundant infrastructure and alerting that wakes a human when something looks wrong. We publish incidents openly on our status page instead of pretending nothing ever breaks.

Security: isolation by design

Every KVM VPS runs as a fully isolated virtual machine with its own kernel, and every LXC VPS is an unprivileged, resource-limited instance with its own root filesystem, network and process space. Administrative access to our infrastructure is encrypted and restricted, and we operate under GDPR obligations for all customer data. Curious what full isolation means in practice? Read our guide on KVM vs container VPS.

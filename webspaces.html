Why InstantNode

Everything your website needs

  1. Free SSL, always on

    Automatic HTTPS for each website and subdomain, issued and renewed for you. You never touch a certificate.

  2. Hard resource limits

    RAM, CPU, disk and process count are enforced per site with a cgroup slice and disk quota. Change your allocation live, with no downtime.

  3. True tenant isolation

    No customer can see another tenant's files, credentials or processes. This comes from a locked-down web root, a hidden process list, private temp and a root-only secret store.

  4. Live usage and history

    Watch RAM, CPU, disk and processes update live in your panel. A history chart shows you when it is time to size up.

  5. Threat-actor finder

    A built-in scanner scores your site for miners, scanners, web shells and rogue cron jobs. It is tuned to keep false positives low.

  6. SSH and SFTP access

    You get full SSH and SFTP access into your web root. There's also a web file manager and a one-click SSO into the panel. Manage your site however you like.

We always have the answers

Web hosting FAQ

Still unsure? Our support team answers on Discord and by ticket.

Which payment methods can I use?
You top up your InstantNode balance via PayPal, credit card or cryptocurrency. Each service is then paid from that prepaid balance. Webspaces renew monthly and you can cancel any time.
Do I get SSH access?
Yes. Each webspace gets SSH and SFTP access into your web root. You also get a web file manager and config editors in the panel, plus a one-click SSO into the full admin. Manage your site however you prefer.
Which apps can I install?
The app store installs real, working apps in one click. Choices include WordPress, Nextcloud, Moodle, Laravel, Matomo, phpMyAdmin and over 25 more, plus GitHub starter templates. Each install hands you the site URL plus its admin and database credentials.
Are other customers isolated from me?
Yes. Each webspace runs in its own cgroup slice with a disk quota. The platform is hardened, so no customer can see another tenant's files, credentials or processes. A built-in scanner also flags miners, web shells and other abuse.
Can I change my plan later?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. The new RAM, CPU and disk limits apply live, with no downtime and no reinstall. You can watch your usage in the panel to know when it is time to size up.
Is SSL really free, and how fast is setup?
SSL is free and automatic on each website and subdomain, and it renews itself. Webspaces are provisioned right after checkout, so your hosting - including SSL and the panel - is ready within minutes.
